- Serverless mode now syncs with your account in both directions: chats, messages and attachments made on the device are uploaded, and chats added or changed on the Web (or other devices) are downloaded. Sync runs automatically a few seconds after changes and when you return to the app; "今すぐ同期" in the "接続" card runs it on demand. Automatic sync on mobile data follows the device cache setting.
- "サーバーのAPIキーを端末へ取り込む" copies the API keys saved in your account to this device after identity confirmation.
- Chats created with "サーバーを使わずに始める" can be imported into your account from the "接続" card.
