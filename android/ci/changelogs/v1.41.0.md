- Changed serverless mode to read chats from the server instead of keeping a full copy on the device. Answers are still generated on the device with your own API keys, and each question and answer is saved to your account as soon as the answer finishes (including stopped answers and errors).
- When the server cannot be reached, you can keep sending: the answers and any new chats stay encrypted on the device and are saved to your account automatically when the connection returns. The "接続" card shows how many messages are waiting and keeps "今すぐ同期" for sending them right away.
- The automatic sync option and its mobile data restriction were removed, because only finished answers are uploaded now.
- After updating, chats that earlier versions copied to the device are removed from it; messages that had not been sent yet are kept and uploaded.
