# V3.9.5: The Grand Finale (Complete Reconstruction)

**Date:** 2025-12-31
**Status:** LTS (Long Term Support)

This update marks the completion of the V3.x series reconstruction, ensuring feature parity with V2.9.9 while integrating advanced V3 security and AI features.

## 🌟 Feature Complete

### 🧠 Advanced AI Capabilities
*   **Gemini 3.0 Series:** Full support for Pro & Flash, including **Thinking Process** visualization and **Nano Banana Pro** (Image Generation) with `thought_signature` management.
*   **GPT-5 / Grok 4:** Enhanced reasoning content extraction (`model_extra`) for deep thinking models.
*   **Thinking UI:** Dedicated collapsible box for inspecting AI reasoning steps.

### 🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security
*   **Full E2EE:** End-to-End Encryption for **Chat History**, **System Prompts**, and **Uploaded Files (Images/PDFs)**.
*   **File Isolation:** Uploads are stored in protected `instance/` storage, accessible only via authenticated streams.
*   **Secure Deletion:** Data is shredded (overwritten) upon deletion, making recovery impossible.

### 🎨 Refined UX
*   **Setup Wizard:** Streamlined onboarding with Legal/Privacy checks and E2EE toggle.
*   **File Library:** Full file management system with icon previews for non-image files.
*   **Visual Polish:** Smooth animations for modals, buttons, and message loading.
